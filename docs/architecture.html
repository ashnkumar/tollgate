Where the two numbers come from
count_tokens gives the input size before anything is spent — free, and documented as an estimate. max_tokens is the absolute maximum on output, which is what makes the ceiling real.

services
The provider's rate card and output ceiling.
calls
One escrow, plus its own frozen copy of the terms it was funded under.
balances
Earnings, refunds and the platform cut. Withdrawn, never pushed.
The buyer's key never reaches the server. The escrow transaction and the redemption signature are the two things that have to come from the buyer, and both are made on their side.
The settler key does not hold the earnings. It reports usage; the money accrues to the provider address, a separate account here. The contract permits a provider to register itself as its own settler, so this separation is how the demo deploys rather than a rule it enforces.
